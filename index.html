Carmilla is a lovely read!

If i am choosing favorites, Carmilla is within the top two; and its not number two. Carmilla is one of the first pieces of gothic literature containing vampires.To answer your question, yes this book came before the infamous Dracula novel.

Carmilla takes place in the 19th century. It begins with our protagonist Laura describing the events of her childhood. She recounts one childhood dream that everyone else denies but she is convinced it did. After twelve years, she finds out the dream was a reality.

Without spoiling the entire book, it explores the vampire-human relationship between Carmilla and Laura. It begins as a blossoming friends between two girls and further grows into an obsessive an possessive relationship between the two as Laura becomes more aware of Carmilla’s vampiric nature.
